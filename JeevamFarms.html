About Jeevam Farms

For the past four years, Jeevam Farms has been cultivating paddy using Shri Subhash Palekar’s Natural Farming (SPNF) method. Our farming relies entirely on indigenous cow-based inputs, including cow dung preparations and Panchagavya, following completely chemical-free and regenerative practices.

Preserving Indigenous Rice Varieties

India is home to hundreds of ancient indigenous rice varieties, many of which are now on the verge of extinction. At Jeevam Farms, we consider it our responsibility to preserve these traditional varieties.

  • Some varieties are grown for seed conservation
  • Others are cultivated for nutritious rice consumption

Why Indigenous Organic Rice?

Indigenous rice varieties typically have lower yields and require more care, which results in slightly higher prices. However, they offer superior nutrition and play a vital role in protecting soil health and biodiversity.

Today, soil organic carbon levels in India have dropped from an ideal 2% to around 0.2%, leading to a severe decline in micronutrients in food grains. Supporting natural farming is essential to reverse this trend.

Supporting organic and natural farming means protecting our soil, our food, and our future.
