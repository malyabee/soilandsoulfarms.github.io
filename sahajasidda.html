Farm Story

BMK Sahaja Siddha Farm was born from a simple but powerful idea — to grow food in harmony with nature and provide safe, chemical-free food for people.

After observing the harmful effects of chemical farming on soil, water, and human health, the farmer decided to return to traditional natural farming practices. The goal is not just to grow crops, but to restore soil life, protect biodiversity, and create a sustainable farming ecosystem.

This farm believes that healthy soil produces healthy food, and healthy food creates healthy people.

Natural Farming Philosophy

Natural farming is a chemical-free, sustainable farming approach that works in harmony with nature. It avoids synthetic fertilizers and pesticides and focuses on using local, farm-based resources. The goal is to build a self-sustaining ecosystem where soil, plants, animals, and microorganisms work together to support healthy crop growth.

Sentropic & Natural Farming Practices

No Chemicals

Completely avoids chemical fertilizers and pesticides.

Soil Regeneration

Focus on improving soil life using microorganisms and organic matter.

Mulching

Soil is covered with crop residue to conserve moisture and improve fertility.

Biodiversity

Multiple crops and natural ecosystems improve resilience and productivity.

On-Farm Inputs

Uses locally available resources like cow dung and urine for inputs.

Minimal Disturbance

Soil is not disturbed heavily, preserving natural microbial life.

Natural farming methods rely on ecosystem balance, soil microorganisms, and minimal external inputs to sustain productivity and environmental health. [oai_citation:1‡Testbook](https://testbook.com/articles/natural-farming?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Natural Inputs Used

  • Jeevamrutham: A microbial solution made from cow dung and urine that improves soil fertility.
  • Ghanajeevamrutham: Solid form used for soil enrichment.
  • Neemastram: Natural pest control solution.
  • Brahmastram: Herbal pest management solution.

Jeevamrutham is rich in beneficial microorganisms and helps improve soil fertility and crop productivity. [oai_citation:2‡World Scientific News](https://worldscientificnews.com/jeevamrut-a-natural-fertilizer/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
