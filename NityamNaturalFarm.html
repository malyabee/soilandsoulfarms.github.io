From Code to Cultivation — A Journey Back to Nature

🌱 A Journey from Code to Soil

For over two decades, Siva Prasad was part of the fast-paced software industry. While his career grew, the demanding lifestyle began to affect his health.

Seeking change, he embraced a natural, chemical-free way of living. The transformation was profound and life-changing.

What began as a personal shift soon became a mission to grow clean, trustworthy food for others.

🌾 Returning to Roots

Leaving behind the corporate world, Siva Prasad returned to his village in Andhra Pradesh and established Nityam Natural Farm across 8 acres.

  • Natural farming based on Indian traditions
  • Focus on soil regeneration
  • Encouraging biodiversity
  • Seasonal and mindful cultivation

Respect the soil, and it will nourish life.

❤️ A Father’s Promise

At the heart of Nityam lies a deeply personal story.

Siva Prasad’s son, who is on the autism spectrum, follows a strict Ayurvedic lifestyle that requires food completely free from chemicals, preservatives, and reused oils.

When such food was difficult to find, a decision was made — to create it without compromise.

This led to the birth of Nityam Natural Snacks.

🍃 What Makes Nityam Special

  • Naturally Grown Ingredients – Chemical-free produce
  • Traditional Oils – Only unrefined oils used
  • Zero Reuse Policy – Oil used only once
  • Farm-to-Family – Same food served to their own family

🌶️ Crops & Specialization

Grown naturally in the rich soils of Andhra Pradesh.
