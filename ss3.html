BMK’s Sahaja Siddha

A journey from family food needs to living biodiversity

Living tree canopy and natural biodiversity at BMK’s Sahaja Siddha

The Beginning: Farming Without a Name

BMK’s Sahaja Siddha did not begin as a project or a business. It started quietly, without a name, under the guidance of Beegala Malakondaiah, with one simple objective:

  • To provide pure and safe food for the family

In the early phase, cultivation was limited to nearly 1 acre of land, growing rice, vegetables, fruits, and trees. All farming was done without chemicals, guided by traditional wisdom and respect for soil as a living system.

A Turning Point – 2018

In 2018, a conversation with neighbouring farmer Penchalaiah revealed the heavy use of pesticides and chemical fertilizers in modern farming. Many farmers themselves avoid eating their own produce due to chemical contamination.

  • Land expanded from 1 acre to 2.5 acres
  • Protection walls and mesh fencing were built
  • Commitment to chemical-free farming was strengthened

Naming the Farm – 2019

In 2019, the farm was formally named BMK’s Sahaja Siddha.

  • Sahaja – Natural, in harmony with nature
  • Siddha – Achieved through lived experience

COVID-19 Period: Focus on Biodiversity

During the COVID-19 period, farming evolved beyond crops to include biodiversity, ecological balance, and soil health.

  • Nakshatravanam (star-based plantation)
  • Vinayaka Chavithi pooja plants
  • Medicinal plants, native and perennial trees

Organic fields and diverse cropping landscape at Sahaja Siddha

Tree & Plant Diversity

Major Trees & Perennials

  • Neem, Jackfruit, Custard Apple (Seethaphal)
  • Ramaphal, Lakshman Phal, Hanuma Phal, Lakshmi Phal
  • Bael (Maredu), Bamboo, Vippa
  • Coconut, Pepper vines
  • Taiwan Ginger, Noni fruit

Fruit Plants & Specialty Crops

  • Guava – nearly 20 varieties
  • Mango – 10 varieties
  • Peanut Butter Plant, Papaya
  • Bananas: Amruthapani, Karupuravalli, Bangla, Yalaki, Rasthali, Nendran, Blue Banana, Batti Green Banana, Old Green Banana
  • Rudraksha, Allspice plant, Biryani leaf

Five-Layer Farming Model – 2020

In 2020, a five-layer farming model was implemented on 1.5 acres, integrating tall trees, medium trees, shrubs, climbers, and ground cover crops, enhancing biodiversity and long-term soil health.

Organic Rice Cultivation Expansion – 2025

  • Rice cultivation started on 50 cents
  • Expanded by another 50 cents in the same year
  • Total organic rice cultivation reached 1 acre

Traditional rice varieties such as Kalabatti, Mysore Malliga, Bahurupi, Sivansamba, and other indigenous varieties were grown completely without chemicals.

What BMK’s Sahaja Siddha Represents Today

  • Biodiversity-rich natural farming
  • Soil treated as a living system
  • Seed self-reliance
  • Respect and dignity for agriculture

What began as a family food requirement has today become a quiet movement growing in harmony with nature.
